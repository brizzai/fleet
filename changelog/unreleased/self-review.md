**Review your agent's work without reading every line.** Press `v` on a session or checkout: a fresh reviewer turns the checkout's changes into a one-line verdict, the few decisions only you can make (each with its code and what was already checked), and the files you can skip with the reason why. Answer with `y`/`n`, press `S`, and your answers go back to the session you pick as its next message.
