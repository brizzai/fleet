**Jira, alongside Linear.** `Ctrl+K` → "Connect Jira" with your site, email and an API token, add `{"jira": {"project": "OPS"}}` to a repo's `.fleet.local.json`, and typing `OPS-42` in the `w` dialog now names the branch, writes the ticket and its screenshots into the worktree, moves the issue to In Progress, and opens the agent already told to read it. `t` shows one merged list across both trackers.
