New terminal drawer (press `` ` ``): a collapsible panel of plain shells — dev servers, log tails, scratch commands — scoped to the selected repo/worktree, sharing the preview column. You type straight into the shell; `Ctrl+T` opens a new one, `Ctrl+W` closes, `Ctrl+PgUp/PgDn` switch tabs, `Ctrl+G` attaches full-screen.
