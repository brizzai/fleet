**Snooze the noise** — `z` mutes a session, worktree, or repo so work you've decided not to touch today stops claiming a slot in the `Space` rotation and stops counting toward the header pills. Pick `30m`/`1h`/`4h`/tomorrow 9am, or type your own (`15m`, `30h`, `2d`) — the picker shows the exact wake time before you commit. The session keeps running and keeps its real status; snoozing a repo dims and folds the whole group, including sessions you create in it later. Press `z` again to wake it early.
