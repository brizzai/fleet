**Read and answer pull requests in fleet.** `c` lists the reviews waiting on you and shows each one the way its own page opens — title, branches, labels, checks and the author's description, with no wait — then `⏎` opens a full-screen diff reader with syntax colours, word-level highlighting and inline comments. `1` is a tour Claude writes through the change, `2` the diff, `3` the agent working on it; `S` posts your comments back as one GitHub review, and what you've read and written survives a restart.
