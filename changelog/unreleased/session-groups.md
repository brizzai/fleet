**Session groups** — When a session spins up worktrees in other repos (`fleet wt` through the fleet skill), those sessions and the one that launched them now share one sidebar section instead of scattering across three repos. Use `.` → "Move to Group…" to group sessions yourself, `R` on a group header to rename it, and `d` on it to delete the whole group's sessions and worktrees.
