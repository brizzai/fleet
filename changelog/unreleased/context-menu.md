**Press `.` for the menu.** A context menu now opens on the row under your cursor, listing exactly the actions that apply to it — a session, a worktree, a repo, and an origin each get their own list. Actions that exist but can't run right now stay visible and dimmed with the reason (`Fork to Worktree — Claude only`), and every entry shows its real keybinding, so you can keep pressing `.` or graduate to `d`/`Y`/`r` whenever you like.
